# Agents: the composer, workstreams, the chat pane

The harnesses run for real and cost real tokens. One or two sessions, tiny
prompts ("add a line to notes/todo.md"), and read the logs rather than re-run.

- The docked composer: harness, model, effort and permission pickers; `@` for
  files; sending.
- A workstream card on the board: does it show progress, and open its chat?
- The floating composer from a card's plus: is the card attached?
- Permission prompts: approve and deny. Does the file change match?
- What happens when a harness is missing or fails to start?
